# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant v2.1 to maintain a welcoming, harassment-free community.

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards
**Please do:** use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community.

**Please do not:** engage in discriminatory language or imagery, harass or intimidate, publish others' private information without permission, or act in a way that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
- Report issues via a GitHub issue addressed to `@ilyafedotov-ops`.
- The maintainer will review and respond in good faith as promptly as possible.
- Consequences may include temporary or permanent restrictions from participation.

## Scope
This Code applies within all repository spaces and when representing the project in public channels.
